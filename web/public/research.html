2026 • Erzurum + Riva • VR + Performance

Measurable Performance Change with VR

Erzurum, 3-5 Apr 2026 — Design: 9th International Exercise and Sport Psychology Congress. Poster, Ethics No: 1565. System: 10 VR exercises, ball speed 40-130 km/h, reaction, visual tracking, selective attention.

Riva, 13-15 Oct 2026 — Results: 5th International Football and Science Congress (FUBI 2026). 20 goalkeepers, age 14-17, 8 weeks. VR group: -8.10 sec improvement, p=0.004. Control: no significant change. Results as reported by the team; peer-review status to be stated separately if pending.

What It ProvesMeasure — Verify — Pay logic was tested in VR. No verification, no result / payment.
What It Does NOT ProveWe do not use VR in dental treatment. This is a goalkeeper performance study, not a clinical dental study.
